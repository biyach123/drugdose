About DrugDose

Study, knowledge and educational discovery.

Our Mission

DrugDose organizes useful educational links so learners can explore different subjects and topics from one place.

Our Vision

To make learning resources easier to discover through a simple and accessible platform.

Our Approach

We connect learners to external educational platforms. Those platforms own and maintain their content.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does Study Program work?

Enter a topic, select a category and open the relevant search links.

Does DrugDose host every article or video?

No. It provides links to external educational websites and topic searches.

Is the calculator a medicine dose calculator?

No. It performs general arithmetic and standard unit conversions only.

Are all external resources free?

Many resources are free, but some external websites may require an account or payment.

Does DrugDose provide medical advice?

No. It is an educational platform, not a substitute for a qualified healthcare professional.

Privacy Notice

Search information

Study topics are used in your browser to create external search links. Do not enter private or sensitive personal information.

External websites

When you open an external link, that website may process information according to its own privacy policy.

Calculator

Calculations are performed in your browser. Do not use the calculator to make medical decisions.

Disclaimer

DrugDose is an educational resource directory. It does not provide diagnosis, treatment, prescriptions, medicine dose calculations or personalized medical advice.

External links are provided for learning and discovery. DrugDose does not control or guarantee the accuracy, availability or content of third-party websites.

For personal medical questions, consult a qualified doctor or pharmacist.
